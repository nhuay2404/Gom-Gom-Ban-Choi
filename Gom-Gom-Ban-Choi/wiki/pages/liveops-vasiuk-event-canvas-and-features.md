# LiveOps Event Canvas and event features

Source: [[liveops-vasiuk-src]], ch. 10, pp. 123-145.

**Event definition**: a time-limited experience combining core gameplay with new mechanics, able to tune economy or retention on the fly without a developer or patch (p. 123). Event blueprint: Theme, Progression, Promotion, Monetization.

## 11-element canvas (Business Model Canvas adaptation; online at vasi.uk)
Four groups, filled in four steps (pp. 134-135):
1. **Goal setting**: Purpose (1-2 sentences; e.g. drain surplus currency after a previous event, or win back after summer), Success criteria (specific, quantifiable, time-bound: e.g. -10% hard-currency balance with +5% hard-currency purchases; reconnect 2,000 lapsed; +18% matches or +6% DAU), Visual references.
2. **Design**: Concept (elevator pitch plus expected player journey), Mechanics, Scope (**MVP for first-time events; expand if proven**).
3. **Environment**: Players (target segment; do not depend solely on it), Constraints (legal: Belgium loot boxes, China minors' hours; cultural: green hat example; Time/Resources/Quality), Risks (could also be opportunities; assess qualitatively and quantitatively).
4. **Time frame**: Schedule (teaser, pre-launch, launch; how late joiners still benefit; spending period may extend past the event), Communication plan (consistent identity, triggered messages, CTA).
Use for initiation (2-4 h workshop), overview/briefing (1-2 h), management (tracking against criteria); teams of 4-12; keep an "Idea Incubator".

## Event features (pp. 137-145)
- **Tournaments** (leaderboards: collection, speedrun, win-streak; time-boxed and friend/geo boards; notifications to regain rank).
- **Minigames** (diversify play, test mechanics; some graduate to permanent modes).
- **Self-challenging** (Need for Speed Unbound side bets let weaker content shine).
- **Temporary collections** (limited items, display, tracking page, completion bonus; endowment effect).
- **Real-world data** (Matchday predictions).
- **Progression** (individual or collective milestones); **Promotion** (share to social with event-currency reward).
- **Event currency and event shop** (points instead of fixed rewards give choice; prefer spending in-game so players open the client; direct purchase from the event page reduces friction but costs dev). Rewards passive (auto) or active (claim).

## Stance and applicability
- (a) PC multiplayer with web event pages, tournaments and real-world feeds; heavy planning process suited to a team of 4-12 and many stakeholders.
- (b) Process template from the authors' practice; success-criteria numbers are illustrative, not benchmarks.
- (c) Complements [[concept-spreadsheet-modeling]] (feature design docs) and [[concept-live-ops-and-inflation]] (events as sinks). Contradicts "ship events as pure rewards": prefers event currency plus shop; compare the single-reward Battle Pass pacing in [[concept-battle-pass-vip-gacha]].
- (d) **Medium-high.** A one-page purpose + success-criteria + scope + schedule brief, MVP-first scope, and "late joiners can still benefit" are cheap and strong. Collections with a tracking page and event-currency shops suit a deco game (themed furniture sets). Tournaments/real-world data are low fit.
