# Engagement as a chain: loyalty, advocacy, UGC

Source: [[liveops-vasiuk-src]], ch. 8, pp. 84-92.

- **Engagement = a run of positive emotions** that yields loyalty, self-adoption (cross-purchase without hard selling), active selling (advocacy), co-creation. Negative experiences cut the bond.
- **Four boosters of LTV**: (1) loyalty/co-development (Riot PBE, Warframe TennoCon, ESO ticketing); (2) cross-promotion via self-adoption rather than marketing push (Blizzard, Valve examples); (3) advocacy (players tell ~5-10 people about good experiences, 10-15 about bad; only exceptional experiences create buzz; measure referral conversion, CPA, NPS); (4) UGC (mods, streams, memes; creator metrics: popularity, retention, time played, as in UEFN).
- **Engagement over time is a heartbeat** (pp. 89-90): baseline (installs and retention curve) + updates + live services in between. D1-3 driven by FTUE, D7-14 by meta and variety, **D30+ mostly by social**. Baseline decays as incoming cohort quality declines (look at retention per time cohort). Dev updates are expensive; supplement with self-expression, momentum and team involvement.
- **Team engagement (p. 90)**: staff who do not play their own game cannot engage players; leadership by example; owning mistakes (CD Projekt refund offer on Cyberpunk).
- Whales get club treatment (private community managers) but the studio should also include friends and family (Wargaming Fest Tanker Day, 250k attendees, p. 91). Pokemon GO example: initial engagement drivers also created problems with non-players.

## Stance and applicability
- (a) Social multiplayer with community, esports and creators; assumes meaningful word-of-mouth and fandom.
- (b) Anecdotes (large-brand cases) and generic claims; the 5-10 vs 10-15 word-of-mouth ratio is folklore-grade. Retention-by-day driver mapping is plausible and common industry belief.
- (c) Compatible with [[concept-progression-systems]] and the D-N retention reading in [[concept-analytics-and-kpis]]. Qualifies the D30 driver: for solo casual games "social" is usually thin, so D30 depends on meta/deco progression ([[concept-core-loop-and-metagame]]).
- (d) **Medium.** Useful: retention-by-day diagnostic, "revenue follows engagement", cohort decay awareness, team playing its own game. UGC/mods low for us; player-owned cat/home customisation and shareable rooms are the casual analogue (see [[liveops-vasiuk-peoples-liveops-and-attention]]).
