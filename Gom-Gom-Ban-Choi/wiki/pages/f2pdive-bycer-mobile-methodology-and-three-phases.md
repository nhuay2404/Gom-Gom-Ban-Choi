# Mobile methodology, daily loop and the three phases

Source: [[f2pdive-bycer-src]], ch. 5.4-5.5, 4.2, 7.3, 9.4.1.

## Mobile methodology (5.4)
- Sessions of 1-5 min; the loop must be quick enough for any moment but deep enough to return to. Too long loses pick-up-and-play, too short burns content.
- Throttles: energy (slow regen, buy more) or the **daily loop**: quest/mission lists (play X stages, use a gacha banner, fight a player) that pay premium currency once per day; randomized daily quests (three at random, reroll one) whose rewards far exceed normal play, so the optimal behaviour is "finish quests, stop".
- Goal: daily routine; players in routine keep playing and eventually paying.

## Three phases (5.5), all must be complete at launch
1. **Onboarding/tutorial**: lock unneeded systems and UI, teach every major system (including monetization; often the last tutorial is the gacha/monetized mechanic), re-tutor on each unlock, grant the most resources here.
2. **Daily loop**: tutorial wheels off; rewards slow a little; more daily quests than needed so players choose; the account level unlocks modes.
3. **Late game**: slowest rewards; optimisation for hardest content; PvP rankings/guilds or PvE raids/expert content; this hardcore group spends most. Late game is not an "endgame": it never ends.
Time per day falls as players move from phase 1-2 to 3, except competitive games. A weak phase kills retention in that phase. Author's warning: a big first month followed by 90% loss next month = failed game; retention is the key live-service metric.

## Game modes (7.3, summary)
Main stage mode (chapters, rising difficulty, boss stages with extra rewards, no brutal spikes), tower (endless floors), puzzle mode (fixed layouts; doubles as advanced tutorial), roguelike daily mode, co-op raid, day-of-week modes, and a "dollhouse/display case" mode for hero collectors: chibi characters, furniture and decor to buy, visitable by others, least time needed, sometimes tied to resource generation (Figure Fantasy, Arknights base). Each mode should reward a specific progression resource; new gameplay systems may be added later (unlike new monetization).

## Daily-loop ethics (9.4.1)
Opinion: daily loop is exploitative if it grows to consume time or if missing a day loses essential currency. Alternatives: weekly or monthly goals, daily goals pooled over ~3 days, auto-play/sim to shorten dailies; ideal daily loop about 30 min, max 1 hour. TF2's weekly drop cap is the model of non-punishing design.

## Stance and applicability
(a) Hero-collector / PvP mobile and PC live service; "daily loop" as the retention backbone for 30-60 min/day players. (b) Phase model and mode list are observational/documented; the 30 min and "two weeks of play = invested" rules of thumb are opinion with no data. (c) Compare [[concept-core-loop-and-metagame]] and [[concept-progression-systems]] (Czerkawski: loops and journey curve, no mandatory daily), [[concept-analytics-and-kpis]] (GD Fresher: D1/D7/D30 retention is measured, Bycer is qualitative), and [[concept-pacing-and-content-control]] (energy as pacing). (d) Fit: high for onboarding structure (progressive unlock + re-tutorial per system, generous early rewards) and for the display/"dollhouse" mode, which is close to our Deco metagame; medium for the daily loop (use gentle, pooled, non-punishing dailies). Phase 3 content for a casual match-3 is the long level map plus deco areas and live-ops events.
