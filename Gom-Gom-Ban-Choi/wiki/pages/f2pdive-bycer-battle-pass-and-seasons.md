# Battle passes and seasonal play

Source: [[f2pdive-bycer-src]], 10.4, 6.4, 7.2, 9.4.4, 10.3 (Warframe, Path of Exile as non-pass contrast).

## Description (10.4)
- A battle pass is a season-length progression (typically 1 month or more) with tiers unlocked by completing challenges (win, play the loop, specific tasks) that award points; a purchased pass unlocks a premium track with exclusive items and more currency than the individual price. First major example: DOTA 2 in 2013; now near-universal. Typical price $14.99-19.99 (= a month of subscription or an expansion). Paying premium currency to get the pass often returns that currency when the pass is completed.
- Benefits (opinion + documented): recurring, predictable revenue; focuses content planning; gives daily reasons to play; players get a lot for one flat price; free tier gives all players new content.
- Criticisms: pass-only items are unobtainable otherwise; free tracks with empty tiers are "making the experience worse"; pressure to play daily; unfinished pass = money lost. Proposed fairer variant: pass as a pseudo-expansion permanently attached to the account with no time limit; DbD, Azur Lane, Arknights keep season story/challenges accessible afterwards without the pass rewards.
- Contrast: monthly resource pass (daily resources) is less effective and not tied to gameplay; Warframe and Path of Exile need no pass: everything earnable, only cosmetics/speed-ups for money.
- Seasons in PvP: ranked resets, per-rank rewards ([[f2pdive-bycer-pvp-and-game-modes]]).

## Stance and applicability
(a) PC/mobile multiplayer live service and hero-collector games with seasonal content. (b) Dates and prices documented; fairness arguments opinion. (c) Compare [[concept-battle-pass-vip-gacha]] (GD Fresher: detailed pass design, tier pacing, conversion) and Czerkawski ch. 4 (pass as retention with time pressure, [[concept-monetization-strategies]]), which treat time pressure as a feature; Bycer treats expiry/daily pressure as an ethical cost and offers the permanent-pass variant, and rejects empty free tiers. (d) Fit: medium-high. A seasonal deco/cat pass or event pass suits a casual match-3 + deco game; adopt his guardrails: no empty free tiers, generous window (or catch-up), keep earned items after season, and pass rewards cosmetic rather than level-skipping.
