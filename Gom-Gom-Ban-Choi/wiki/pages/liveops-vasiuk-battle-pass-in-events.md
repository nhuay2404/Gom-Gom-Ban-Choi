# Battle Pass as an engagement mechanic

Source: [[liveops-vasiuk-src]], ch. 10, pp. 141-144; also p. 115.

- **Reframing**: in F2P with few payers the pass is "a monetized engagement mechanic" (retention first, direct revenue second). Many players treat it as their only repeatable purchase (p. 141). Poorly suited to UGC-centric environments (Roblox) where tasks cannot be coherent.
- **Free track**: attainable goals, less lucrative than paid but compelling; include in-game currency so regulars drift to premium content and later purchases.
- **Challenges**: variety for different player types; avoid forcing a single class or mode; in team games, team objectives credit individuals; length should match content depth; avoid repetitive play (Destiny 2 PvE/PvP mix).
- **Catch-up**: overlapping task objectives let latecomers clear several in one session; do not make paid level purchases the primary catch-up; discount level purchases near season end; Halo Infinite non-expiring passes cut end-of-season fatigue but reduce FOMO.
- **End-of-season**: recurring challenges or rewards once players max out; teasers for the next season.
- **Teamwork**: shared objectives, no forced party play, solo players not penalised.
- **Rewards**: strong first and last rewards; mix small frequent and large milestone rewards; some exclusives. **Hard currency in the pass**: none makes it feel weak; too much lets players renew forever for free; cap it so saved gems plus play earnings cover only the next pass if they bought one (or give the next chapter free but not the whole season).
- **Drivers**: FOMO and perceived efficiency; instant exclusive reward after purchase reduces regret; Fortnite ties time-limited events to pass-only content to show non-payers what they miss (p. 143-144).
- **Upsell**: tier skips (retroactive rewards), mission reroll, event passes, subscription bundles, gifting passes.
- **Cannibalisation watch**: ARPU, ARPPU, conversion, spend distribution, first-purchase rate, LTV by cohort, resource inflow/outflow.

## Stance and applicability
- (a) Fortnite/Call of Duty-style seasonal pass in a competitive multiplayer game.
- (b) Design heuristics from named games; no measured conversion data. The hard-currency renewal loop is a genuine and well-known economy hazard.
- (c) Extends [[concept-battle-pass-vip-gacha]]. **Qualifies**: tier-skip upsell and "show non-payers what they miss" lean toward the pressure patterns in [[concept-dark-game-design-patterns]]; the non-expiring (Halo) option is less manipulative but weakens FOMO. Cannibalisation KPIs link to [[concept-ab-testing-monetization]].
- (d) **Medium.** Free track with currency, strong first/last reward, retroactive pass activation, and the hard-currency renewal cap apply to a casual season pass. Team design guidance is moot; mission variety maps to level/deco/cat tasks.
