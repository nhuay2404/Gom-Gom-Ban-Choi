## One-paragraph thesis
A game economy is a controlled, simplified fabrication of a real economy whose job is long-term engagement: it brakes content distribution, controls session length, gives a reason to return, and provides monetization hooks. It is designed top-down (core loop → metagame features → numbers in spreadsheets), balanced by simulation then by live telemetry, and monetization must be built in from the start, not bolted on.

## Ideas most relevant to a mobile match-3 / casual game (our project)
These are my pairings, not the author's claims.
- Pinch points, lives/energy and rewarded video ([[concept-monetization-strategies]]) map directly onto a level-based puzzle loop.
- Soft-currency sinks and "infinite" consumable sinks ([[concept-live-ops-and-inflation]]).
- Spreadsheet simulation of progression and drop tables ([[concept-spreadsheet-modeling]]) for level/coin pacing.
- Starter pack, bundles, SKU table and price anchoring ([[concept-store-and-pricing]]).
- Day-N retention, conversion, ARPDAU, LTV ([[concept-analytics-and-kpis]]).
