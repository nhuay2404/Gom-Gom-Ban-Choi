# Personalization, sandbox and community (Bycer)

Source: [[cozydive-bycer-src]] ch. 3.5, 5.3 (pp. 77-82), 3.2 (diorama games), 6.

## Distinctions
- **Customization** = choices that affect ability/options; **personalization** = choices that change look to fit style (3.5). Stardew has both; cozy leans on personalization (clothes, decorations, naming animals, painting buildings).
- **Diorama games** (3.2): full personalization of a space with no resource limits and typically no goals.
- Personalization gives a stress-free creative outlet and a place free of criticism (3.5); screenshots of Animal Crossing islands and invitations to visit drove its reach during lockdown (documented in his account).

## Sandbox mode (5.3)
- Sandbox = all loss states and survival pressures switched off (like all difficulty cheats at once). Survival/crafters have the most examples; best practice is to ship a ready preset rather than only sliders.
- Test questions: does the game offer varied personalization, do mechanics afford creative use, and is a harder game appealing to casuals?
- Sandbox widens audience; Minecraft "would have never achieved the same success" without creative mode (opinion).
- Open-ended is not sandbox: Stardew still needs money and skill levels to see everything; mods that remove that are sandbox.
- Offer multiple ways to play (factory games: basic vs Rube Goldberg builds) with no "correct" way and no punishment.
- Never underestimate players given tools (Minecraft redstone, Build the Earth); but anything required must be onboarded.
- Quantity of options: players always want more; modding/SDK and Steam Workshop extend it; moderate mod content in multiplayer casual settings.
- Marketing: put personalization front and centre in trailers.
- Community: wikis, guides, multiplayer and sharing extend life (3.5).

## Stance and applicability
(a) PC/console sandbox and survival-crafting; modding and Workshop are PC assumptions. (b) Mostly documented examples; value judgments on sandbox and audience are opinion. (c) Complements [[concept-monetization-strategies]] (personalization is the "preference/personalization" monetization category, noted as the most content-treadmill-prone in [[concept-pacing-and-content-control]]); Bycer discusses only the design appeal, not selling cosmetics, and says players always want more options - which supports the treadmill warning. Modding and SDK do not apply to our Capacitor build. (d) Fit: high for the deco meta: free rearrangement with no resource penalty, naming/dressing cats, gift-like deco, shareable screenshots (consider a share/photo mode), a "no-pressure" creative zone separate from the puzzle's fail state, and multiple valid ways to furnish a room. Check budget: variety demand grows without end.
