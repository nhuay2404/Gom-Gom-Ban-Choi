# Case studies: Marvel Snap and The Finals

Source: [[src-czerkawski-game-economy-design]], ch. 7 (pp. 157–169). The author stresses these are deductions from public products, not insider knowledge.

## Marvel Snap (F2P mobile CCG) — "clean and concise" metagame
- Core: 12-card deck, 3 locations, 6 rounds, win 2 of 3 locations.
- Loop: **Battle** gives Boosters (card-specific, for a random deck card), daily/season mission XP and Credits; **Upgrade** spends Boosters + Credits, raising card rarity (cosmetic effect) which raises **Collection Level**.
- Progression: **Collection Level** (main; unlocks cards by Series 1–5, predefined starter cards for onboarding, then mystery cards; gacha caches can give currency instead of cards, slowing distribution), **Season Pass** (new card each season, level-timed mission sets control pace, endless cache loop for free players), **Ranks** (Cubes won/lost; seasonal cosmetics).
- Monetization: hard currency **Gold** (small free amounts too). Uses: cosmetic **variants** (share the base card's Boosters, so more upgrade chances but you still must play), buy Credits with Gold (daily limit), **daily mission refill** (monetizes the Credits source but demands more play), special offers.
- **Pinch point**: Boosters are guaranteed from play; Credits are scarce. Fast-upgrade store (5 Credits per Booster) gives progress spikes but is inefficient long term, deepening the Credit squeeze.
- Season Pass is bought with real money (not Gold), so Gold doesn't fund the next pass; this keeps spend tracking per currency clean.
- Content cycling: a new card is premium via the pass, then filtered into normal distribution and becomes cheaper.
- Hand-crafted expensive bundles sit at eye line in the store.

## The Finals (F2P PC/console shooter) — personalization only
- Soft currency VRs (capped, forcing spending) unlocks weapons/gear; item XP levels unlock skins; Career level gives cosmetics; Ranked Tournament leagues give cosmetic rewards. Free cosmetics complement paid ones.
- Store = featured subset of the inventory; bundles-only purchase for bundled items; standalone = bundle of one; seasonal starter bundle; price varies by item and bundle ([[concept-store-and-pricing]]).
- Battle Pass: 1150 Multibucks (~$10) or 2400 (with first 20 levels unlocked, ~$20); reaching the last level returns 1575 Multibucks, enough for next season's pass.
