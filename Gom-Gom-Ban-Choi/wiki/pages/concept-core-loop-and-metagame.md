# Core loop and metagame

Source: [[src-czerkawski-game-economy-design]], ch. 2 (pp. 23–29).

- Design approach: see the whole experience, prototype quickly, test, iterate. Do not polish one section first (chess analogy: prototype with placeholder pieces).
- **Core loop** = repeated cycle of an *action phase* and *rest* (menus). The economy is felt in the repeated loop; the intent is habit.
- To draw it: list resources/goods, map sinks and sources and the purpose of each area. Example "Project X" (mobile PvP shooter): Battle phase gives XP, Cash (soft), upgrade tokens; Upgrade phase spends Cash + tokens; Gold (hard) buys Battle Pass, extra weekly missions, token packs. A separate Monetization block complements the loop. Keep it high level: exact XP numbers belong in the design docs.
- No standard format; draw it so the team understands it. Analyses on Deconstructor of Fun are good study material.
- **Metagame** = systems and loops wrapped around core gameplay that drive long-term engagement (not the eSports "meta"). Economy and metagame overlap almost completely; the economy is what players *feel* across the metagame systems.
- Outer loops grow around the core loop during live ops ([[concept-live-ops-and-inflation]]).
