# Personalization across the lifecycle: onboarding, retention, winback, monetization

Source: [[liveops-vasiuk-src]], ch. 11, pp. 145-156.

- **Stack**: predictive modelling, rule engine (segment + trigger + timeframe), placements; clean **target vs control groups** make personalization the most measurable LiveOps area. A strong emotional moment (a hard-won victory) can matter more to an offer than segment or discount (p. 147).
- **Onboarding** (pp. 147-149): no history, so no predictive models; treat all newcomers with maximum care. Funnel for PC: register, launcher, download, login, tutorial, first game; chain of reminder messages, add a reward if plain messages fail; tailor to referral/influencer/country; in-game hints on first-time actions, educational tasks with rewards.
- **Retention** (pp. 149-151): detect churn-risk while still active (in-game channels work best). Churn features: falling own activity (veterans cycle out and back), late unlocks vs personal pace, gap patterns, hardware. Tools: **self-expiring content** (e.g. premium days, no stock-piling), achievable missions, personal outlet with proxy currency, **personal daily rewards** (equal or escalating; hidden maximum optional), meaningful discount for fiat-invested players. Reach recently lapsed quickly ("iron while hot"); a consolation bonus after a loss is acceptable.
- **Winback** (pp. 151-153): newbies churn permanently, so contact fast with understandable value (soft currency, premium time, hyped character; not obscure boosters); engaged churners return organically, so do not spam early; tools: reminders, "did you know", announcements, test drives, rewards for target actions, challenges; message chains with varied timing; protect the active base ("do not offer more value to lapsed than active").
- **Monetization** (pp. 153-156): purchase probability and revenue-forecast models; segment table: new non-payer (find the **50% churn day and median sessions before first purchase** to time first-offer), regular non-payer, ex-payers (longer gap = bigger discount), active payers (**avoid discounts to active payers**; watch ARPPU). Offers: discount, exclusive content (do not give event-grind items away in a daily offer), personal challenges, retargeting for browsed items, action points (bonus on purchase), indirect monetization (hard-currency leftovers spent more easily). Target active audience in-game; do not bombard newbies or players about to leave; let players buy from the popup.

## Stance and applicability
- (a) PC/console mid-to-hardcore with launcher/email, predictive ML team; examples are WoT-style premium time and vehicles.
- (b) Practitioner playbook; the only quantified items are identification windows to compute; no uplift data.
- (c) Aligns with [[concept-price-discrimination]] (segment pricing) and [[concept-ab-testing-monetization]] (target vs control). **Strongly differs** from common casual practice of discounting to everyone: he says avoid discounts for active payers and big ones only for long-gap ex-payers. Daily reward guidance is permissive (equal or escalating) and does not discuss streak-loss pressure; see [[concept-dark-game-design-patterns]]. Ads-based casual segmentation is not covered ([[concept-iaa-ads-design]]).
- (d) **Medium-high.** Time-to-first-purchase window, newbie-appropriate offers, self-expiring rewards, "no discounts for active payers", and returning-player protection are portable with rule-based segments. Email/launcher channels are not; mobile push is.
