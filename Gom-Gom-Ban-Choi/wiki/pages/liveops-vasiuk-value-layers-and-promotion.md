# Four layers of value and promotion rules

Source: [[liveops-vasiuk-src]], ch. 5 pp. 46-48; ch. 9 p. 117.

- **Value = functional + financial + intangible + emotional** (p. 46-47).
  - Functional: features and utility (stats, speed).
  - Financial: real-world price and in-game resource yield (premium account, pass).
  - Intangible: rarity, comfort, confidence, trust (try-before-you-buy, easy cancellation, refund-if-disappointed).
  - Emotional: belonging, glory, purpose, support for a cause or a favourite creator.
- **Willingness to pay**: propositions built on functional + financial value are priced by utility and rarely exceed historical price levels (commodities: consumables, currencies). Adding intangible and emotional value (insurance, creator skin, charity) is what raises conversion and quantity without inflating utility (p. 47).
- RPG example: a replaceable scout character sells at utility price; add a one-week money-back guarantee plus an emotionally meaningful skin and it sells at higher conversion and price.
- **Commodity vs premium purchase journeys** (p. 116-117): commodities have short consideration, sold in the client next to the loop that needs them (one-click). High-value items have long research (community, reviews) so need a short trackable path from the community into the store, and promotion should lean on emotional and intangible value.
- **Promotion copy**: as simple as possible, one key message and a call to action; visuals emphasise the main idea; leave room for decoy/cognitive bias in layout; influencers often carry more trust than in-game UI (p. 117).

## Stance and applicability
- (a) Midcore PvP/collection game where premium items (vehicles, skins) are big-ticket; consumables are "commodities".
- (b) Conceptual (marketing theory), no measured uplift for the emotional layer.
- (c) Extends [[concept-reward-design-and-psychology]] and the offer anatomy in [[concept-offers-and-merchandising]] (%OFF vs %VALUE) with a non-price lever. Compatible with [[concept-iap-packs-and-starter-pack]] but says commodities cap out at utility price, which tempers hopes of lifting booster-pack ARPPU by framing alone.
- (d) **High for deco.** Decoration items are cosmetic/emotional goods: sell the cat's story, the home, a theme, rather than "50% more". Commodity side (boosters, coins) stays utility-priced and in-loop.
