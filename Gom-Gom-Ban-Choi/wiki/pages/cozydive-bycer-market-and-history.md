# Cozy market and history (Bycer)

Source: [[cozydive-bycer-src]] ch. 2.2, 3.1-3.3, 3.5-3.6, 6.

## Lineage (documented facts, as he tells them)
- Early low-stakes: SimCity (1989, built as a sandbox), The Sims (2000, dollhouse), Harvest Moon (1996/97, no combat or fail state), Rune Factory (2006, adds combat), Animal Crossing (2001; New Horizons 2020 boomed in lockdown), Minecraft creative mode (2011), Katamari, A Tale in the Desert, Second Life.
- Casual market: PopCap (Bejeweled, Peggle, PvZ; PvZ praised for GUI), Zynga/Farmville and Rovio/Angry Birds (2009), browser games built on real-world timers. See [[cozydive-bycer-real-time-and-daily-play]].
- 2010s indie wave: To the Moon, Dear Esther ("walking simulator" stigma), Stardew Valley (2016, ~41M copies by one estimate), Slime Rancher, My Time at Portia, Forager (cozy + idle), Coffee Talk, Spiritfarer, Unpacking, A Short Hike, PowerWash Simulator, diorama games (full personalization, no goals), Venba, Wanderstop, Pokopia (2026).
- 2024 trend: quarter-view idle games (Rusty's Retirement) - low input, runs beside other work.
- 2019: Wholesome Games / Wholesome Direct (Matthew Taylor) gave the market a label and a showcase (3.3).

## Market arguments
- ESA 2025 figures cited: 68% play to relax, 62% for fun, 21% play with a disability (2.2). Cozy is accommodating to disabilities.
- Bycer's view: an underserved audience exists but is niche; a hit like Stardew does not mean 40M cozy fans exist (3.6). Following hits produce "worse versions" (farming clones, bullet-heaven clones). Genre mash-ups target fans of "A+B", not A and B (3.6).
- Adding complexity to a beloved cozy base loses the people who loved it (3.6).
- AAA has barely entered cozy because the personal touch is hard at scale (6.2). Sustainability (budget to market size, 4.4) is a recurring concern.
- Two play-pattern archetypes (3.5): short story (hours) vs long-term sessions not meant to be long. Length is not quality (5.4).
- Will cozy change? His hope: more games that are easy to start then grow, and fewer that equate cozy with "incredibly simple" (6.2).
