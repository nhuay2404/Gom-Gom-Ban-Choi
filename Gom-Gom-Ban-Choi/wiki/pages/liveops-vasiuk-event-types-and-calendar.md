# Four event types and the LiveOps calendar

Source: [[liveops-vasiuk-src]], ch. 4, pp. 37-40; calendar waste points ch. 12.

- LiveOps goal is value added by events **outside key development updates**. Four classes:
  1. **Repeatable** (game at the centre): daily/weekly/monthly. Tweak parameters to stabilise content consumption and economy: currency conversion rates, weekend specials, **XP multipliers that wash out soft currency**. Exploit in-week seasonality (more time at weekends) and in-month (payday). Polish and repeat them.
  2. **Personal** (player at the centre): predictive analytics plus emotional triggers (e.g. "gained epic medal"); each player has a personal cycle that may contradict the seasonal event (a heavy-retention event in low season while an active player wants discounts).
  3. **Special** (culture/region): sport, festivals, holidays; set a theme for weeks or months; content-rich; can feed season passes.
  4. **Out-of-game** (human at the centre): streamers, BizDev tie-ins, movie releases, to remind players of the game when not playing.
- Typical big spikes: Christmas, Halloween, Black Friday.
- **Calendar** (pp. 39-40): one shared calendar for all four types. **Planning horizon: 1 month of specificity plus 2 months of open-to-interpretation** (ambition with flexibility; lead time for content and contracts). **Balanced tempo**: modulate intensity to avoid burnout; "more of the same" is not enough, give unexpected value while letting players rest. Catalogue of repeatable events with descriptions; personal offers with trigger/condition metadata.
- Ch. 5 adds: players can face event overload (updates, regional news, surveys, community events) and isolated or irrelevant communications; sport finals or a new show can be threat or opportunity (pp. 41, 50).
- Ch. 12 adds the **inventory waste** example (p. 164-165): a long queue means events planned far ahead; if event A overperforms on revenue, a pre-planned revenue event B right after it will underperform; also two contradictory events at once (one drains a currency, another floods it) is a defect (p. 166).

## Stance and applicability
- (a) Assumes a PC/console live service with a dedicated team, regional markets and many segments. "Repeatable" events are tuned by tweaking exchange rates and multipliers.
- (b) Practitioner experience (Wargaming); no measured cadence data. The 1+2 month horizon is a heuristic.
- (c) Aligns with [[concept-pacing-and-content-control]] (burn vs starve, cycling content) and [[concept-live-ops-and-inflation]] (XP multipliers/soft-currency sinks). Does not give an events-per-week number; compare the GD Fresher deck ([[src-gd-fresher-f2p-monetization-training]]) for offer cadence. Contrast: Vasiuk warns against stacking revenue events back to back, whereas many casual puzzle teams run overlapping events; he supports it only if they target different segments.
- (d) **Medium.** The taxonomy, shared calendar, "do not queue events far ahead", and "do not run an event that floods the currency another event drains" are directly usable. Personal events need real data scale; a small game can do a simple version. Out-of-game events are low value for us.
