# Hero collectors, rarity tiers, and balancing paid content

Source: [[f2pdive-bycer-src]], 8.5, 11.3, 9.4.4, 10.1.

## Hero-collector design (8.5)
- Extension of the MOBA champion model, attachable to any loop (RPG, tower defense, match-3). Mobile versions lean on native or licensed IP because brand recognition sells. Rarity tiers: 1-2 star common (given free), 3-4 average (free through play), 5-6 top (banner rewards; modern games give one free); or C/R/SR/SSR/UR/LR. Minimum three tiers. Higher rarity = better base stats and inherent value; traditional games avoid rarity so every character stays viable.
- Adding a new top tier years later reads as a cash grab; do it as a big event with few characters (Azur Lane UR in 2020; Dragon Ball Legends Ultra after 3 years).
- Duplicate-pull upgrades favour spenders: offer a free alternative upgrade currency. Quality over quantity of new characters; rerun old banners (banner minimum two weeks). Best characters sometimes locked behind long grind quests (Marvel Strike Force raids) rather than purchase.
- New-account hacks: reroll accounts until a good free character appears; large starter pulls (20-100).
- Team building spreads power so no single character decides; avoid rigid class compositions. New characters should not be strictly better, or so good they are mandatory. Balance patches after sale need compensation. FOMO: limited banners and yearly seasonal characters; author quit a game after missing one.

## Balancing live content (11.3)
- Abstracted progression gives infinite numbers, but raising stats changes nothing about play; prefer content that changes gameplay (new warframes, characters, maps, modes) over number inflation. Never ship strictly better items: use trade-offs and conditional effects (his two-card example: Card 2 is higher damage but decays each turn).
- PvE: calibrate new content to what free/guaranteed players hold, not the top 10%. PvP: no arms races. Balance fixes (Hearthstone Miracle Rogue: raise the combo card's cost) are better than nerfing strong options; never sell the solution to a problem you created (Marvel Strike Force revive counter on a legendary). Put complexity on rarer items so power is earned by skill. Never paywall fixes or balance patches.

## Stance and applicability
(a) Gacha hero collectors on mobile and PC character-based live service. (b) Examples documented; balance advice is design opinion with good internal logic, no data. (c) Complements Czerkawski's rarity and perks discussion ([[concept-progression-systems]], [[concept-reward-design-and-psychology]]) and GD Fresher's gacha ([[concept-battle-pass-vip-gacha]]); the "no strictly better item" and "content should change gameplay, not numbers" points qualify Czerkawski's comfort with vertical progression/inflation ([[concept-live-ops-and-inflation]]). (d) Fit: medium. We have six cat types and deco items rather than heroes; apply: variety by trade-off not power, new boosters/cats/deco as sidegrades, rare tier introduced slowly with events, no FOMO-only characters, and always a free upgrade path.
