    How to play

    Each turn you draw one card. A card holds 1–3 cats in a single, I or L shape.

    Tap the current card to rotate it, then drag it onto the board. Highlighted cells show where the card will land.

    Boards come in different sizes and shapes (squares, hearts, triangles, diamonds…). From level 11 you unlock the “Hold” slot: drag a card into it to stash it (or swap it with the stashed card), as often as you like, and tap it to take the stashed card back. There are 6 cat breeds: orange, gray, white, tuxedo, Siamese and tabby. You only score when you match 3 or more adjacent cats of the same breed (they disappear): 3 = 30, 4 = 50, 5 = 80, 6+ = 120 points. Peek at the next cards to plan ahead. Crates block cells and can't hold cats; match cats next to a crate to break it. Each level has its own target score and move limit; running out of moves or space means you lose. From level 3 you get boosters that don't cost a move: the Hammer smashes one cat or crate, New card swaps your current card, and +3 gives extra moves. When you run out, tap one to buy it with coins.
